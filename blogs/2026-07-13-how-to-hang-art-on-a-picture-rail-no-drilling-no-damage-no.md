If your flat has one of those thin wooden strips running around the top of the wall, just below the coving, you've already got the easiest hanging system going — you just might not have clocked it yet. Picture rails were fitted into most Victorian and Edwardian houses as standard, and a lot of period conversions and flats still have them, even after everything else has been modernised. They were designed for exactly one job: hanging art without touching the plaster.
