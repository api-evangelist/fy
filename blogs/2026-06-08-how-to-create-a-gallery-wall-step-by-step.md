The short version: A gallery wall is a curated group of prints, art, or photos hung together as one arrangement. Choose a wall you actually look at, pick a layout (grid, salon, or linear), build around one anchor piece, plan it on the wall with paper templates before you drill, and centre it at roughly 140–145cm from the floor.
