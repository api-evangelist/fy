If you're staring down a refurbishment or a new-build handover and "art" is still an empty line on the spec sheet, you're not alone. It's one of the last things procurement teams price properly, and one of the first things guests notice when it's missing or wrong. The good news: sourcing art for hospitality and build-to-rent doesn't need to be complicated, and it doesn't need a six-week consultancy engagement to get a number you can actually put in a budget.
