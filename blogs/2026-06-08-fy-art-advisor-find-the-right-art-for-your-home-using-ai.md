The Fy! Art Advisor is a free AI-powered tool that recommends wall art based on your room, your style, and your colour palette. Describe your space in words, upload a photo of your wall, or paste a link to something that captures the vibe you're after — and the Advisor builds you a set of curated art boards from Fy!'s catalogue in seconds.
